# NEXUSLEND CHURN INTERVENTION PLAN
## Problem
Financial strain is associated with materially higher churn risk, with township customers requiring particular care because a recall gap can conceal unmet need.

## Solution
Pilot **Fee Waiver for High-Strain** alongside two opt-in supports: load-shedding support packages and flexible payment plans. No customer is penalised for declining assistance.

ETHICAL ALERT: Do NOT target high-strain customers with predatory offers. Support must be affordable, opt-in, transparent, and available without a penalty for declining.

## ROI
- Projected ROI: R2.1M net value across 3 interventions.
- Lead intervention: Fee Waiver for High-Strain; investment R185,000, 320 customers saved, ROI 1024%.
- Inclusion reach: 224 of 320 eligible customers in underserved communities (70%).
- Per CFO: 'Every 1% churn reduction = R420k saved'
- Per Head of Inclusion: Relief must be measured by uptake and outcomes in underserved regions, not only by aggregate profit.

## Next Steps
- Launch a time-boxed pilot with transparent eligibility, opt-in consent, and affordability review.
- Retrain if township recall drops >10%.
- Review monthly uptake, complaints, repayment outcomes, and inclusion reach by region.
